About Mathura & Vrindavan

Mathura and twin holy town Vrindavan form the spiritual heart of Braj Bhoomi along the sacred Yamuna River, revered across the world as the birthplace and childhood playground of Lord Krishna.

From the ancient Garbha Griha sanctum at Shri Krishna Janmasthan and riverside Vishram Ghat to illuminated white marble Prem Mandir, ecstatic kirtans at Banke Bihari and ISKCON Vrindavan, sacred groves of Nidhivan, and Govardhan’s Kusum Sarovar, Braj immerses every visitor in timeless bhakti.

Top Places to Explore in Mathura & Vrindavan

Sacred Krishna temples, Yamuna river ghats, Braj kunjs & archaeological treasures

Shri Krishna Janmasthan Temple Complex 📷 12 Photos
Deeg Gate, Mathura, UP
Sacred Birthplace

Holiest shrine in Mathura built around the historic Garbha Griha prison cell believed to be Lord Krishna’s birthplace, alongside the majestic Bhagavata Bhawan temple.

Prem Mandir, Vrindavan 📷 17 Photos
Chhatikara Road, Vrindavan, Mathura, UP
White Marble Shrine

Spectacular 54-acre white Italian-marble temple carved with intricate Krishna Leela panels, illuminated each evening in glowing colors beside choreographed musical fountains.

Shri Banke Bihari Mandir 📷 9 Photos
Bihari Pura, Vrindavan, Mathura, UP
Sacred Temple

Beloved 1864 shrine established by Swami Haridas’s lineage where thousands gather daily for ecstatic glimpses of Thakur Banke Bihari Ji’s tribhanga idol.

Sri Krishna Balaram Mandir (ISKCON) 📷 14 Photos
Raman Reti, Vrindavan, Mathura, UP
Spiritual Sanctuary

Iconic pure white-marble complex in Raman Reti founded by Srila Prabhupada, globally celebrated for non-stop uplifting Hare Krishna kirtan and devotional beauty.

Shri Dwarkadhish Temple 📷 14 Photos
Vishram Ghat Precinct, Mathura, UP
Historic Haveli Shrine

Grand 1814 riverfront temple renowned for elaborate Rajasthani stone carvings, painted ceilings, and colorful Hindola swings during Shravan and Janmashtami.

Vishram Ghat 📷 7 Photos
Yamuna Riverfront, Mathura, UP
Sacred Yamuna Ghat

Principal holy ghat of Mathura where Lord Krishna rested after defeating Kansa; illuminated nightly with hundreds of floating earthen lamps during evening Yamuna Aarti.

Nidhivan, Vrindavan 📷 13 Photos
Near Banke Bihari, Vrindavan, UP
Mystic Sacred Grove

Enigmatic lush grove of intertwined holy tulsi trees and Rang Mahal shrine wrapped in timeless Braj devotion and the nocturnal Raas Leela legends.

Kusum Sarovar, Govardhan 📷 12 Photos
Govardhan Parikrama Road, Mathura, UP
Heritage Step Tank

Monumental 450-foot sandstone reservoir framed by ornate 18th-century Jat memorial chhatris whose serene arches mirror clearly into emerald sacred waters.

Shri Radha Rani Temple, Barsana 📷 10 Photos
Bhanugarh Hill, Barsana, Mathura, UP
Hilltop Shaktipeeth

Palatial red-and-white sandstone temple crowning Brahmachal Hill at Srimati Radharani’s birthplace, famous globally for Barsana’s joyous Lathmar Holi.

Shri Radha Raman Temple 📷 9 Photos
Panchayati Chauraha, Vrindavan, UP
1542 Heritage Shrine

One of Vrindavan’s ancient Sapta Devalayas established by Gopala Bhatta Goswami enshrining the self-manifested Saligram murti of Radha Raman Lal.

Shri Radhavallabh Lal Ji Temple 📷 9 Photos
Gotam Nagar, Vrindavan, UP
Ancient Sandstone Shrine

Historic Vrindavan shrine founded by Hith Harivansh Mahaprabhu renowned for ornate sandstone craftsmanship, morning Mangal Aarti, and rich Braj bhajan traditions.

Mathura Birla Mandir (Gita Mandir) 📷 14 Photos
Mathura–Vrindavan Road, UP
Gita Stambh Shrine

Graceful red-sandstone Lakshmi Narayan temple featuring manicured gardens and the Gita Stambh pillar engraved completely with all 18 chapters of the Bhagavad Gita.

Government Museum, Mathura 📷 13 Photos
Dampier Nagar, Mathura, UP
Archaeological Museum

Founded in 1874, showcasing India’s premier collection of Kushana, Shunga & Gupta red-sandstone sculptures from the celebrated ancient Mathura School of Art.

Kans Quila 📷 12 Photos
Yamuna Northern Bank, Mathura, UP
Ancient River Fort

Atmospheric historic fortress perched above the Yamuna River traditionally linked to Kansa’s citadel and later fortified by Raja Man Singh of Amber and Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh.

Baba Jai Guru Dev Ji Mandir 📷 12 Photos
NH-19 Agra–Delhi Highway, Mathura, UP
White Marble Dome

Striking pure white marble memorial temple featuring symmetrical domes, delicate carvings, and wide landscaped lawns along the highway south of Mathura.

Shri Bhuteshwar Mahadev Ji Mandir 📷 10 Photos
Bhuteshwar, Mathura, UP
Kotwal Shiva Shrine

Venerated ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva as one of Mathura’s four sacred guardian Kotwals, sheltering the subterranean Patal Devi cave shrine.

Shri Dauji Maharaj Temple 📷 10 Photos
Baldeo, Mathura District, UP
Lord Balarama Shrine

Historic shrine enshrining a colossal black stone idol of Lord Balarama (Dauji) with Revati Mata, celebrated for exuberant post-Holi Huranga festivities.

Shri Raman Bihariji Mandir 📷 13 Photos
Mathura, Uttar Pradesh
Devotional Temple

Serene devotional landmark adorned with traditional Braj stone arches and vibrant sanctum décor inviting quiet darshan and soulful evening kirtans.

🚗 How to Reach Mathura & Vrindavan

By Air: Nearest major airports are Agra (~60 km) and IGI New Delhi (~165 km via Yamuna Expressway).
By Train: Mathura Junction (MTJ) is one of North India’s busiest rail hubs connecting Delhi, Mumbai, Agra, Jaipur & Varanasi.
By Road: Fast access via the 6-lane Yamuna Expressway & NH-19.

🍲 Local Flavors & Specialties

Taste authentic Mathura sattvik prasadam and Braj dairy specialties:

  • Mathura Peda: Iconic caramelized milk-khoya fudge flavored gently with cardamom — sold fresh around Dwarkadhish & Vishram Ghat.
  • Dubki Wale Aloo & Bedai: Piping hot crispy bedai puris served with tangy spiced potato curry at Brijwasi & Holi Gate.
  • Kullhad Lassi & Rabri: Thick creamy curd topped with malai and saffron served in earthy clay cups.

✨ Insider Travel Tips

  • Night Lighting: Visit Prem Mandir after dusk (6:30 PM onward) to witness its color-shifting lighting and musical fountain show.
  • Ghat Aarti: Reach Vishram Ghat before sunset for serene Yamuna boat rides and lamp aarti.
  • Nidhivan Timings: Remember Nidhivan gates close strictly by dusk after Sandhya Aarti.
