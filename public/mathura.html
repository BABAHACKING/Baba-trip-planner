Mathura

The Birthplace of Lord Krishna

About Mathura

Mathura is a sacred city in Uttar Pradesh, believed to be the birthplace of Lord Krishna. It is dotted with temples and is a major pilgrimage center for Hindus, especially during Janmashtami.

Top Attractions

Temples and Ghats in Mathura

Krishna Janmabhoomi

The temple complex built around the prison cell where Krishna was born.

Dwarkadhish Temple

One of the oldest and largest temples in Mathura.

Vishram Ghat

A bath and worship on the banks of the river Yamuna.

Govardhan Hill

A sacred site associated with the legends of Lord Krishna.
