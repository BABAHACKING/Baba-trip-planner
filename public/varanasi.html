About Varanasi

Varanasi — revered eternally as Kashi and Banaras — stands along a crescent curve of the sacred Ganga as one of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited cities and the spiritual crown jewel of India.

From early morning sunrise cruises connecting Assi Ghat and Manikarnika Ghat to the grand marble Kashi Vishwanath Corridor, evening fire rituals at Dashashwamedh Ghat, Buddhist stupas at neighboring Sarnath, and royal riverside heritage at Ramnagar Fort, every lane in Kashi feels alive with devotion, classical music, and craft.

Top Places to Explore in Varanasi

Sacred Jyotirlinga, riverfront ghats, Buddhist stupas & historic temples

Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple & Corridor 📷 8 Photos
Vishwanath Gali, Varanasi, UP
12 Jyotirlinga Shrine

Supreme sanctuary of Lord Shiva adorned with gold-plated spires and connected via the grand stone Vishwanath Dham Corridor directly down to the holy Ganga riverfront.

Dashashwamedh Ghat 📷 5 Photos
Ganga Riverfront, Central Kashi, UP
Grand Ganga Aarti

Varanasi’s most vibrant historic ghat steeped in Puranic lore, coming alive every dusk with the synchronized brass-lamp fire rituals, Vedic chants, and floating diyas of Ganga Aarti.

Assi Ghat 📷 5 Photos
Southern Ghat Belt, Varanasi, UP
Sunrise & Culture

Beloved southern riverfront ghat where Assi meets Ganga, celebrated for Subah-e-Banaras classical dawn music, yoga, sunrise boat launches, and relaxed riverside cafes.

Manikarnika Ghat 📷 4 Photos
Old Kashi Riverfront, Varanasi, UP
Eternal Sacred Ghat

Ancient sacred Mahashamshana ghat revered in Hindu cosmology as the gateway to moksha, flanked by historic stone shrines and centuries-old riverside architecture.

Dhamek Stupa, Sarnath 📷 14 Photos
Sarnath (10 km NE of Varanasi), UP
Buddhist Heritage

Towering 43-meter Gupta-era cylindrical brick and stone stupa marking the sacred Deer Park site where Gautama Buddha preached His very first Dharma sermon.

Ramnagar Fort 📷 15 Photos
Eastern Bank of Ganga, Varanasi, UP
Royal Fort & Museum

Mid-18th-century Chunar-sandstone citadel built by Kashi Naresh Raja Balwant Singh overlooking the river, housing vintage royal cars, palanquins, weaponry, and an astronomical clock.

Shri Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple 📷 5 Photos
Lanka, Varanasi, UP
Sacred Shrine

Venerated temple established by Goswami Tulsidas in the 16th century along Assi stream, cherished for peaceful darshan, monkeys, and famous besan laddoo prasad.

Shree Durga Mata Mandir (Durgakund) 📷 9 Photos
Durgakund, Varanasi, UP
Nagara Red Temple

Striking 18th-century red-ochre multi-tiered Nagara shrine beside the stepped Durga Kund tank, holding deep spiritual reverence across Navratri and Shravan festivals.

Shri Satyanarayan Tulsi Manas Temple 📷 8 Photos
Durgakund Road, Varanasi, UP
Marble Heritage Shrine

Gleaming white marble temple constructed at the historic spot where Goswami Tulsidas composed the Ramcharitmanas, featuring the epic’s verses engraved all across its walls.

New Vishwanath Temple (BHU Birla Mandir) 📷 8 Photos
Banaras Hindu University Campus, UP
Marble Campus Spire

Soaring 250-foot marble Shiva temple planned by Pt. Madan Mohan Malaviya inside leafy BHU lawns, showcasing serene halls inscribed with Bhagavad Gita teachings.

Bharat Kala Bhavan Museum (BHU) 📷 7 Photos
Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi, UP
Art & Archaeology Museum

Distinguished university museum founded with Rai Krishnadasa preserving over 100,000 Banarasi textiles, Mughal and Rajasthani miniature paintings, and ancient Gangetic sculptures.

Bharat Mata Mandir (MGKVP) 📷 9 Photos
Vidyapith Road, Varanasi, UP
National Heritage Shrine

Unique temple inaugurated by Mahatma Gandhi in 1936 dedicated to Mother India, featuring an intricate three-dimensional undivided map of India carved from pure Makrana marble.

Nepali Temple (Kathwala Mandir) 📷 6 Photos
Lalita Ghat, Varanasi, UP
Pagoda Wood Heritage

Revered 19th-century Kathmandu-style pagoda shrine crafted from carved Himalayan teak and terracotta overlooking Lalita Ghat.

Shri Batuk Bhairav Temple 📷 4 Photos
Kamachha, Varanasi, UP
Ancient Kashi Shrine

Revered ancient Tantric shrine dedicated to the youthful form of Lord Bhairav (protector of Kashi), tucked amid quiet historic residential lanes of Kamachha.

Tibetan Temple (Sarnath) 📷 4 Photos
Sarnath, Varanasi, UP
Buddhist Monastery

Colorful Himalayan-style monastery and courtyard gardens near Dhamek Stupa embellished with vivid thangka murals, prayer wheels, and tranquil meditation halls.

🚗 How to Reach Varanasi (Banaras)

By Air: Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS) in Babatpur lies 25 km northwest of the ghats.
By Train: Connected across India via Varanasi Cantt (BSB), Banaras (BSBS) & Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Jn.
By Road: Seamless links via NH-19 to Prayagraj (~2.5 hrs) and Mirzapur (~2 hrs).

🍲 Local Flavors & Specialties

Experience unmistakable Banarasi culinary treasures through old lanes:

  • Kachori-Sabzi & Tamatar Chaat: Crisp morning kachoris and spicy earthen-kulhad Tamatar Chaat topped with namakpara around Dashashwamedh.
  • Malaiyyo (Winter Magic): Ethereal saffron-and-pistachio morning milk froth sold only in cool winter mornings in Chowk.
  • Blue Lassi & Banarasi Paan: Creamy seasonal fruit lassis near Manikarnika lane and fragrant sweet Banarasi paan.

✨ Insider Travel Tips

  • Sunrise Boat & Aarti: Take a dawn wooden boat from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika, then return at 6:00 PM for Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti.
  • Sarnath Half-Day: Plan an unhurried afternoon at Sarnath for Dhamek Stupa, Tibetan Temple, and the Ashoka Lion Capital museum.
  • Temple Comfort: Leave bags and electronics inside hotel lockers or clock-rooms prior to Kashi Vishwanath darshan.
