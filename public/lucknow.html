Lucknow

The City of Nawabs

About Lucknow

Lucknow, the capital of Uttar Pradesh, is known for its multiculturism, art, cuisine, dance, and music. It is affectionately known as the Golden City of the East and is famous for its Adab and Tehzeeb.

Top Attractions

Heritage sites and parks in Lucknow

Bara Imambara

A historical complex famous for its labyrinth, Bhool Bhulaiya.

Rumi Darwaza

An imposing gateway which is considered a fine example of Awadhi architecture.

Ambedkar Park

A public park and memorial dedicated to Bhimrao Ambedkar.

Hazratganj

The downtown shopping district of Lucknow, the heart of the city.
