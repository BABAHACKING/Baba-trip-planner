About Lucknow

Lucknow, the vibrant capital of Uttar Pradesh along the Gomti River, celebrates centuries of refined Awadhi tehzeeb, intricate Chikankari embroidery, classical Kathak and music, and world-renowned culinary craftsmanship.

From the astonishing pillarless vaults of Bara Imambara and towering Rumi Darwaza to European-fusion wonders like La Martiniere College, atmospheric 1857 heritage at The Residency, and sprawling green waterfronts like Janeshwar Mishra Park, Lucknow rewards every traveler.

Top Places to Explore in Lucknow

Grand Imambaras, colonial landmarks, riverside parks & iconic bazaars

Bara Imambara & Bhool Bhulaiya 📷 16 Photos
Hussainabad, Old Lucknow, UP
Heritage Monument

Built in 1784 by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula, home to the world’s largest unsupported arched hall alongside the mysterious 1,000-passage Bhool Bhulaiya labyrinth and Shahi Baoli stepwell.

Rumi Darwaza 📷 16 Photos
Hussainabad Heritage Zone, Lucknow, UP
Iconic Gateway

Towering 60-foot signature gateway of Lucknow constructed in 1784 in ornate Awadhi style crowned by an octagonal floral chhatri overlooking Old Lucknow.

Chota Imambada 📷 13 Photos
Hussainabad, Old Lucknow, UP
Palace of Lights

Gilded-dome monument built by Nawab Muhammad Ali Shah in 1838, famed for dazzling Belgian crystal chandeliers, Persian calligraphy, and intricate ceremonial tazias.

The Residency, Lucknow 📷 17 Photos
Mahatma Gandhi Marg, Qaisar Bagh, UP
1857 Heritage Ruins

Tranquil red-brick complex preserved amid sprawling lawns as a protected national monument recounting the dramatic 1857 Siege of Lucknow.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Memorial Park 📷 18 Photos
Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, UP
Monumental Complex

Grand 107-acre public memorial built in shimmering pink Chunar sandstone, featuring monumental stupa domes, 62 life-size stone elephants, and bronze galleries.

La Martiniere College (Constantia) 📷 17 Photos
La Martiniere Road, Lucknow, UP
Grand Architecture

Magnificent late-18th-century European-Nawabi palace estate founded by Major General Claude Martin beside the Gomti River, famed for Roman statues and fluted towers.

Dilkusha Kothi 📷 16 Photos
Cantonment Road, Lucknow, UP
Heritage Ruins

Picturesque 18th-century English-baroque country house and royal Nawabi hunting retreat surrounded by peaceful flower gardens and octagonal corner towers.

Shah Najaf Imambara 📷 15 Photos
Rana Pratap Marg, Hazratganj, UP
Mausoleum Shrine

Distinguished white-domed Awadhi mausoleum inspired by Najaf, housing the serene resting place of Nawab Ghazi-ud-Din Haidar and his begums amid manicured lawns.

Sikandar Bagh 📷 16 Photos
Ashok Marg, Lucknow, UP
Heritage Garden

Historic walled villa and summer garden developed by Nawab Wajid Ali Shah featuring carved gateway arches inside the National Botanical Research Institute campus.

Hazratganj Market Boulevard 📷 19 Photos
Central Lucknow, UP
Culture & Shopping

Lucknow’s timeless Victorian-arcaded heart built for leisurely evening "Ganjing" walks through heritage bookshops, Chikankari emporiums, theaters, and legendary chaat spots.

Gomti Riverfront Park 📷 15 Photos
Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, UP
Riverfront Promenade

Scenic landscaped promenade stretching along the Gomti River featuring amphitheaters, jogging and cycling tracks, illuminated bridges, and musical fountain evenings.

Janeshwar Mishra Park 📷 15 Photos
Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow, UP
Urban Eco Park

Vast 376-acre green sanctuary inspired by London’s Hyde Park boasting twin scenic lakes, Venetian gondola boats, cycle trails, and lush botanical expanses.

Shri Mankameshwar Temple 📷 8 Photos
Daliganj / Gomti Banks, Lucknow, UP
Ancient Shiva Shrine

Venerable riverside temple dedicated to Lord Shiva along the southern bank of the Gomti, celebrated across Lucknow as a fulfillment shrine during Shivratri and Sawan.

Sri Ramakrishna Math 📷 13 Photos
Nirala Nagar, Lucknow, UP
Spiritual Sanctuary

Harmonious pink-sandstone universal temple blending traditional Indian architectural domes with peaceful meditation halls, prayer shrines, and serene courtyard gardens.

Nawab Wajid Ali Shah Zoological Garden 📷 13 Photos
Narahi, Hazratganj, Lucknow, UP
Wildlife & Heritage Zoo

Centuries-old 71-acre wildlife sanctuary housing white tigers, swamp deer, reptiles, State Museum exhibits, and a nostalgic miniature Bal Rail toy train.

University of Lucknow 📷 13 Photos
Babuganj, Hasanganj, Lucknow, UP
Heritage Campus

Distinguished 1920 Indo-Saracenic academic landmark admired for its stately domed red-sandstone convocation buildings, clock-tower facades, and tree-lined quadrangles.

Phoenix Palassio 📷 17 Photos
Amar Shaheed Path, Gomti Nagar, UP
Modern Architectural Hub

Opulent European-inspired lifestyle palace known for soaring vaulted galleries, grand chandelier halls, dramatic outdoor fountains, and world dining experiences.

Fun Republic Mall 📷 11 Photos
Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, UP
Leisure & Dining

Popular entertainment complex in Gomti Nagar offering cinema screens, family dining lounges, shopping avenues, and lively evening ambience.

🚗 How to Reach Lucknow

By Air: Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport (LKO) connects Lucknow across India and the Gulf.
By Train: Lucknow Charbagh (LKO) & Lucknow Junction (LJN) are major rail hubs served by Shatabdi, Tejas & Vande Bharat.
By Road: Connected via Agra–Lucknow Expressway and Purvanchal Expressway.

🍲 Local Flavors & Specialties

Experience legendary Awadhi royal cuisine and Chowk street treasures:

  • Tunday Kababi & Galouti: Melt-in-the-mouth slow-spiced heritage kebabs served hot with flaky ulte tawe ka paratha.
  • Tokri Chaat & Makhan Malai: Crisp potato basket chaat in Hazratganj and airy winter saffron-milk foam in Chowk.
  • Idris & Rahim Kulcha-Nihari: Classic slow-simmered Awadhi Awadh spices, biryanis, and sweet shahi tukda.

✨ Insider Travel Tips

  • Old City Heritage Walk: Start early at Bara Imambara, Rumi Darwaza, Clock Tower & Chota Imambada — all within a single heritage corridor.
  • Ganjing Evening: Stroll historic Hazratganj in the evening for Chikankari boutiques, vintage architecture, and cafes.
  • Gomti Nagar Sunset: Pair Ambedkar Memorial’s sandstone glow at sunset with Gomti Riverfront musical fountains.
