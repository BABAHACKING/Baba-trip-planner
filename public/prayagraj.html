Prayagraj

The City of Offerings & Sangam

About Prayagraj

Prayagraj, formerly known as Allahabad, is a city of immense historical and religious importance. It is most famous for the Triveni Sangam, the meeting point of three rivers - the Ganges, the Yamuna, and the invisible Saraswati, and for hosting the Maha Kumbh Mela.

Top Attractions

Must-visit places in the holy city

Triveni Sangam

The holy confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati rivers.

Allahabad Fort

A magnificent fort built by Emperor Akbar on the banks of the Yamuna.

Bade Hanuman Ji

A unique temple featuring a reclining statue of Lord Hanuman.

Anand Bhavan

The historic ancestral home of the Nehru-Gandhi family.
