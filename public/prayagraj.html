About Prayagraj

Prayagraj, historically renowned as Prayag and Allahabad, stands among India’s most sacred and historically rich cities at the Triveni Sangam — the mystical confluence of the Ganga, Yamuna, and invisible Saraswati rivers.

From the 16th-century sandstone ramparts of Akbar Fort and the subterranean Bade Hanuman Ji shrine to the Siddha Alop Shankari Shaktipeeth, Mughal-era Khusro Bagh, and the historic Anand Bhawan Museum, Prayagraj weaves timeless spirituality with India’s modern freedom story.

Top Places to Explore in Prayagraj

Sacred shrines, royal forts, freedom-movement heritage & lush colonial parks

Akbar Fort (Allahabad Fort) 📷 9 Photos
Near Triveni Sangam, Prayagraj, UP
Historical Fort

Grand 1583 AD red-sandstone fortress commissioned by Emperor Akbar overlooking the Yamuna–Ganga confluence, sheltering Patalpuri Temple, the sacred Akshayavat tree, and Ashoka Pillar heritage.

Shri Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir 📷 4 Photos
Sangam Bandh Road, Prayagraj, UP
Sacred Shrine

Unique subterranean temple close to Triveni Sangam enshrining a colossal 20-foot reclining idol of Lord Hanuman, miraculously touched each monsoon by the rising waters of Mother Ganga.

Alop Shankari Devi Shakti Peeth 📷 1 Photo
Alopibagh, Prayagraj, UP
Sacred Shaktipeeth

Ancient Siddha Shaktipeeth where devotees revere a sacred wooden swing (dolika) suspended above a holy kund rather than an idol, commemorating where Maa Sati’s hand vanished ("alop").

Anand Bhawan Museum 📷 8 Photos
Tagore Town, Prayagraj, UP
Heritage Mansion

Historic ancestral residence of the Nehru family turned national museum showcasing preserved living quarters, letters, and pivotal memorabilia of India’s independence struggle.

Khusro Bagh 📷 10 Photos
Lukerganj (Near Prayagraj Jn), UP
Mughal Garden & Tombs

Peaceful 40-acre walled Mughal Charbagh garden housing the intricately carved 17th-century sandstone mausoleums of Prince Khusrau, Sultan Begum, and Shah Begum amidst guava orchards.

Allahabad Museum 📷 9 Photos
Chandrashekhar Azad Park, Prayagraj, UP
National Museum

Premier national museum housing rare Kushana and Gupta sculptures from Kaushambi, Harappan relics, Pahari miniatures, Nicholas Roerich canvases, and freedom-movement archives.

Chandrashekhar Azad Park 📷 10 Photos
Civil Lines / George Town, Prayagraj, UP
Heritage Park

Sprawling 133-acre historic green landmark (formerly Alfred Park) honoring revolutionary Chandrashekhar Azad’s supreme sacrifice, dotted with Victoria Memorial canopy and Victorian library halls.

🚗 How to Reach Prayagraj

By Air: Prayagraj Airport (IXD) in Bamrauli connects directly to Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru & Lucknow.
By Train: Major railway junction (Prayagraj Jn / PRYJ) with superfast & Vande Bharat Express connectivity.
By Road: Seamless four-lane highways via NH-19 to Varanasi (~2.5 hrs) and Mirzapur (~2 hrs).

🍲 Local Flavors & Specialties

Savor iconic Prayagraj street delicacies and heritage classics:

  • Netram Kachori-Sabzi: Crispy desi-ghee kachoris paired with spiced potato curry, pumpkin & hot jalebi in Katra.
  • Dehati ke Rasgulle: Legendary melt-in-the-mouth warm black & white rasgullas around Civil Lines.
  • Allahabad Surkha Guava: World-famous crimson-fleshed sweet guavas harvested from Khusro Bagh belt orchards.

✨ Insider Travel Tips

  • Sangam Precinct: Combine Akbar Fort, Bade Hanuman Ji, and the Triveni Sangam riverfront in one relaxed morning.
  • Cultural Double-Bill: Pair Anand Bhawan Museum with neighboring Jawahar Planetarium sky shows.
  • Evening Walk: Explore Khusro Bagh’s red sandstone tombs near golden hour and enjoy Loknath lane chaat.
