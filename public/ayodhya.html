About Ayodhya

Ayodhya, shimmering on the right bank of the sacred Saryu River, stands among India’s seven holiest Mokshadayini cities (Sapta Puri) and is globally celebrated as the eternal birthplace of Maryada Purushottam Shri Rama.

Anchored by the monumental pink-sandstone Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, hilltop Hanuman Garhi, royal Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal, glowing Deepotsav steps along Ram Ki Paidi, and tranquil Guptar Ghat, Ayodhya blends living Ramayana devotion with Awadh’s rich cultural legacy.

Top Places to Explore in Ayodhya

Sacred Ramayana shrines, serene Saryu ghats, ancient palaces & museums

Shree Ram Janmabhoomi Temple 📷 11 Photos
Ramkot, Ayodhya Dham, UP
Supreme Sacred Shrine

Monumental Nagara-style three-story temple built in intricately sculpted pink Bansi Paharpur sandstone, enshrining Shri Ram Lalla at the sacred birthplace of Lord Rama.

Shri Hanuman Garhi Mandir 📷 6 Photos
Sai Nagar / Bhakti Path, Ayodhya, UP
Hilltop Fort Shrine

Grand 10th-century four-sided fort temple perched atop a 76-step mound overlooking Ayodhya; tradition holds devotees first seek Lord Hanuman’s blessings before Ram Darshan.

Kanak Bhawan 📷 13 Photos
Tulsi Nagar, Ayodhya Dham, UP
Royal Palace Shrine

Resplendent "House of Gold" traditionally gifted by Queen Kaikeyi to Devi Sita after marriage, celebrated for golden-crowned idols of Shri Ram and Mata Janaki inside palace courtyards.

Saryu Ghat & Ram Ki Paidi 📷 5 Photos
Naya Ghat, Saryu Riverfront, Ayodhya, UP
Sacred Riverfront

Expansive terraced stone embankments flowing beside the holy Saryu River, illuminated every evening for grand Saryu Aarti and famous globally for record-breaking Diwali Deepotsav.

Dashrath Mahal 📷 7 Photos
Bada Asthan, Ramkot, Ayodhya, UP
Ancient Royal Palace

Revered palace complex commemorating the grand court of Emperor Dashrath where Lord Rama spent His joyful childhood years, echoing all day with devotional bhajans.

Guptar Ghat 📷 9 Photos
Faizabad Cantonment, Saryu Banks, UP
Peaceful River Ghat

Tranquil, shady stone ghat along a wide bend of the Saryu River revered as the sacred spot where Lord Rama completed His earthly sojourn and entered Jal Samadhi.

Shri Nageshwar Nath Mandir 📷 9 Photos
Ram Ki Paidi, Ayodhya, UP
Ancient Shiva Temple

Venerable Shiva shrine situated right beside Ram Ki Paidi, traditionally believed to have been founded by Kush, son of Lord Rama, and thronged during Maha Shivratri.

Mani Parvat Temple 📷 11 Photos
Vidya Kund Area, Ayodhya, UP
Sacred Hilltop View

Historic 65-foot sacred mound steeped in Ramayana and Buddhist antiquity, offering sweeping vistas across Ayodhya’s spires and linked to Hanuman’s flight carrying the Sanjeevani hill.

Ram Katha Park 📷 9 Photos
Naya Ghat, Ayodhya, UP
Cultural Amphitheater

Beautifully landscaped riverside amphitheater and garden hosting traditional Ramleela enactments, devotional festivals, classical concerts, and Deepotsav cultural events.

Imambada Gulab Bari 📷 7 Photos
Vaidehi Nagar, Ayodhya (Faizabad), UP
Awadhi Heritage Tomb

Graceful 18th-century garden mausoleum of Nawab Shuja-ud-Daula set amid historic rose gardens, arched lakhori-brick gateways, and tranquil fountains.

Raj Dwar Mandir 📷 3 Photos
Ramkot Heritage Precinct, Ayodhya, UP
Historic Shrine

Distinctive elevated temple structure standing near the ancient royal gateway zone of Ramkot, admired for its serene courtyards and skyline views.

Durlabh Darshan Kendra 📷 6 Photos
Ayodhya Dham, Uttar Pradesh
Heritage Exhibit

Devotional museum and storytelling gallery bringing timeless episodes from Tulsidas’s Ramcharitmanas to life through detailed sculptural dioramas and murals.

Sunil’s Ramayan Wax Museum 📷 14 Photos
Chaudah Kosi Parikrama Marg, Ayodhya, UP
Cultural Wax Museum

Striking Dravidian-gopuram-styled museum housing dozens of life-size wax tableaux recreating iconic scenes and characters from the epic Ramayana.

Valmiki Bhawan 📷 8 Photos
Chhoti Chhavni / Ramkot, Ayodhya, UP
Sacred Literary Shrine

Peaceful marble-inlaid spiritual hall honoring Adi Kavi Maharishi Valmiki, featuring complete Valmiki Ramayana shlokas carved beautifully across luminous stone walls.

Gurudwara Nazarbagh (Brahmkund) 📷 5 Photos
Nazarbagh, Ayodhya, UP
Historic Sikh Shrine

Sacred Sikh Gurudwara commemorating the historic spiritual visits of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji, and Guru Gobind Singh Ji to Ayodhya.

🚗 How to Reach Ayodhya

By Air: Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ) connects Ayodhya directly with Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru & Ahmedabad.
By Train: Newly redeveloped Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) offers Vande Bharat & Amrit Bharat Express connectivity.
By Road: ~2.5 hrs east of Lucknow along smooth NH-27 highway.

🍲 Local Flavors & Specialties

Enjoy traditional sattvik Awadhi sweets and Ayodhya local treats:

  • Hanuman Garhi Besan Ladoo: Pure desi-ghee aromatic laddoos offered fresh outside the grand steps of Hanuman Garhi.
  • Ram Ki Paidi Rabri & Khurma: Slow-reduced creamy saffron rabri, crisp jalebi, and sweet khurma around Naya Ghat.
  • Guptar Ghat Chana Chaat: Light afternoon snacks and refreshing masala shikanji by the serene Saryu banks.

✨ Insider Travel Tips

  • Darshan Sequence: Follow classic tradition by visiting Hanuman Garhi first before walking along Bhakti Path toward Shri Ram Janmabhoomi.
  • Evening Saryu Aarti: Arrive at Ram Ki Paidi / Saryu Ghat by 5:30 PM to capture illuminated ghats and devotional evening chants.
  • Lockers & Dress: Phone/bag lockers are available along Janmabhoomi Path for smooth temple entry.
