Ayodhya

The Land of Lord Rama

About Ayodhya

Ayodhya is an ancient city located on the banks of the Sarayu River. It is the setting of the ancient Hindu epic Ramayana and is famously known as the birthplace of Lord Rama.

Top Attractions

Holy sites in Ayodhya

Ram Mandir

The grand Hindu temple built at the sacred site of Ram Janmabhoomi.

Hanuman Garhi

A 10th-century temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman.

Kanak Bhawan

This temple is believed to have been gifted to Sita by Kaikeyi.

Sarayu Ghat

Take a holy dip in the Sarayu river, which flows through Ayodhya.
