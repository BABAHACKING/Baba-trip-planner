Agra

The City of Eternal Love

About Agra

Agra is a major tourist destination in Northern India, famous for its Mughal-era buildings, most notably the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and Fatehpur Sikri, all of which are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Top Attractions

Must-visit monuments in Agra

Taj Mahal

An immense mausoleum of white marble, built by Shah Jahan.

Agra Fort

A historical fort in the city of Agra, also known as the Red Fort.

Fatehpur Sikri

A small city in northern India, just west of Agra, founded by a 16th-century Mughal emperor.

Mehtab Bagh

A charbagh complex located north of the Taj Mahal complex.
