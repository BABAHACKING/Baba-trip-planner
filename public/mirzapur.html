About Mirzapur

Nestled along the banks of the holy Ganges and cradled by the ancient Vindhya mountain range, Mirzapur is a hidden gem of Eastern Uttar Pradesh. The district is famous for the sacred Maa Vindhyavasini Shaktipeeth — one of the 51 holy shrines of Goddess Shakti — drawing millions of devotees every year.

Beyond its spiritual significance, Mirzapur is a paradise for nature lovers, boasting magnificent waterfalls like Wyndham Falls and Tanda Falls, the imposing Chunar Fort dating back to 56 BC, and world-renowned handcrafted carpets and brass artifacts. It's the perfect blend of devotion, adventure, and cultural heritage.

Top Places to Explore in Mirzapur

Sacred temples, stunning waterfalls, ancient forts & vibrant culture

Vindhyavasini Temple 📷 5 Photos
Vindhyachal, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Religious Place

Famous 51 Shaktipeeth pilgrimage destination. Millions of devotees visit for auspicious darshan of Maa Vindhyavasini along the revered sacred Trikona Parikrama circuit.

Chunar Fort 📷 8 Photos
Chunar, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Historical Place

Historic fort near the Ganga River dating back to 56 BC. Famous for Sonwa Mandap, Warren Hastings bungalow, ancient sundial, and breathtaking panoramic river ramparts.

Tanda Falls 📷 7 Photos
Tanda, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Natural Waterfall

A tranquil multi-tiered waterfall surrounded by scrub forests and rocky natural steps. Perfect for bird watching, peaceful forest walks, and nature photography.

Ashtabhuja Temple 📷 5 Photos
Vindhyachal Hills, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Religious Place

A revered hilltop shrine dedicated to the eight-armed form of Goddess Durga (Ashtabhuja Devi), accessible by stairs or ropeway with sweeping Vindhya valley views.

Vindham Falls (Wyndham Falls) 📷 6 Photos
Wyndham, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Adventure & Nature

Picturesque natural tiered waterfall nestled in rocky Vindhya crags, with natural stepping pools, forest trails, and scenic picnic viewpoints.

Lakhaniya Dari Waterfall 📷 6 Photos
Ahraura, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Adventure & Trekking

Spectacular deep gorge waterfall hidden within lush green mountain terrain, offering a popular trekking adventure through scenic boulder-strewn canyon trails.

Maa Kali Khoh Cave Temple 📷 4 Photos
Vindhyachal, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Sacred Cave Shrine

An ancient cave temple dedicated to Goddess Kali, located inside a narrow rocky gorge in the Vindhya hills. The second vital point of the sacred Trikona Parikrama.

Pakka Ghat 📷 6 Photos
Ganga Riverfront, Mirzapur
Ghats & Culture

Historic carved stone ghat on the sacred Ganga River. Famed for its ornate sandstone pavilions (chhatris), holy dips, evening aarti, and tranquil boat cruises.

Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower) 📷 5 Photos
Municipal Square, Mirzapur City
Heritage Landmark

Iconic Victorian-Gothic stone clock tower erected in 1891 with exquisite stone carvings. The crowning landmark at the vibrant civic and commercial heart of Mirzapur.

Lal Bhairav Temple 📷 3 Photos
Vindhyachal, Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh
Sacred Shrine

Revered temple of Lord Bhairav (Kotwal of Vindhyachal), traditionally visited by pilgrims to seek protection and blessings to complete the holy pilgrimage.
